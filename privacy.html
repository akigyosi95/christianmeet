Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Christian Meet ("the app") is operated by Alexandru Kigyosi. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what your choices are.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and display name, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Profile: the country, denomination, bio, and profile photo you choose to add. These are visible to other signed-in users of the app.
  • Messages: chats you send are stored so they can be delivered and shown to you and the recipient. Messages are private to the two participants.
  • Social activity: who you follow and which profiles you visit. Profile owners can see that you visited them; visit records are automatically deleted after 30 days.
  • Push token: a device identifier used solely to deliver message notifications, removed when you sign out.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads or share data with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging), acting as our data processor. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

Safety

You can block any user (they can no longer message you) and report users for review. Reports are reviewed by the app team.

Your rights

You can edit your profile and photo in the app at any time. To delete your account and its data, or to request a copy of your data, contact us at the address below and we will action it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
