Common questions

How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap "Forgot password?" — you'll receive a reset link by email.

How do I block or report someone?
Open their profile, tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Block User or Report User. Blocked users can be managed from Profile → Blocked Users.

How do I delete my account?
Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your account and data within 30 days.
